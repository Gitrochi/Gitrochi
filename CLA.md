authorized representative must post the following statement from their own
GitHub account on the official PR, replacing the bracketed fields:

> [permanent URL to the approved CLA.md revision]. My assent covers my original
> contributions in [official PR URL], commits [full SHA or explicit SHA range].

> I have disclosed all third-party material and obtained any required employer
> or other rights-holder permission. I understand that the grant is limited to
> the official Gitrochi project under Gitrochi Community Use License v1.0.

A maintainer must verify the author/rights-holder coverage, approved agreement
revision and exact commits, and retain the comment permalink and verification
record before merge. New commits, added authors, or changed agreement terms
require renewed assent for the affected material. A template checkbox, PR
submission, commit sign-off, silence, or an automated account action is not a
substitute. There is currently no automated CLA enforcement in this repository.

Do not post signatures, home addresses, employment documents or other private
identity evidence in a public PR. Contact both project licensing contacts in
[LICENSE](LICENSE) to arrange private verification if needed. The adoption record
must confirm who may receive, retain and verify such records before collection.

This proposal selects no governing law or exclusive court. Applicable mandatory
